## Notes on derivation

- **Reward-component shares** (`training_reward_and_components.png`): `step_telemetry.jsonl` logs all 7 components directly per step (`reward_components.{reliability,sla,pressure,waste,action,reversal,boundary}`); shares are each component's |mean value| for that session divided by the sum of |mean value| across all 7 components for that session (a stacked-bar composition of magnitude, not of signed value, since several components are often negative).
- **PPO tie-rule frequency**: `ppo_decision.tie_rule_applied` (R1/R3/R4/R5/R6) and `ppo_decision.near_tie` (boolean) are both logged per-decision in `attempt.json`. "How often the tie rule applied" is reported as the `near_tie=True` fraction (an actual tie-break firing, R3/R4/R5/R6) out of all decisions -- R1 ("exactly one action within epsilon of the max") is the normal, no-tie case and is excluded from that fraction, though its count is shown alongside the others in the bar chart for completeness.
- **CPU/memory-per-replica heatmap**: `sample.raw_observation.cpu_per_replica` and `.memory_per_replica` (already per-replica, not raw totals) are used directly, pooled across all eval_final ppo_v2 runs, binned into a 6x6 grid per chosen action.
- **Action names**: `ppo_decision.effective_action_name` uses `autoscaler/src/rl_env/types.py`'s `ACTION_NAMES` convention (`SCALE_DOWN_ONE`/`HOLD`/`SCALE_UP_ONE`), which differs from `scripts/eval_v2_episodes.py`'s own, differently-named `ACTION_NAMES` constant (`SCALE_DOWN`/`HOLD`/`SCALE_UP`) used elsewhere for the normalized per-step action field consumed by the training-curve figures -- both are reproduced faithfully from their respective real source fields, not reconciled into one shared name set.
