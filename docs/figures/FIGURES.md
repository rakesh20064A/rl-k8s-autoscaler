## Note on the PPO policy map
`discussion_ppo_policy_map` shows the DOMINANT action per (cpu_per_replica, memory_per_replica) bin (a 3-color categorical heatmap), rather than 3 separate per-action share files, since Phase 6D found SCALE_DOWN essentially never fires and HOLD dominates almost every bin -- a single dominant-action map is more informative here than 3 mostly-empty share maps.

## Note on reward-component shares
`training_reward_components` reports what `step_telemetry.jsonl` actually logs. If no `reward_component_*` fields are present, the figure says so explicitly rather than fabricating a 7-component breakdown that isn't in the data.
